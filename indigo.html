Give with tax season in mind

One tax season. Several ways to help.

Arizona offers separate income-tax credits for eligible gifts to STOs, public schools, qualifying charitable organizations, and qualifying foster care charitable organizations. You can support more than one category in the same tax year, subject to each category’s limits and your Arizona income-tax liability.

A credit reduces eligible Arizona income tax dollar for dollar; a deduction reduces taxable income. For example, a qualifying $100 gift could reduce your Arizona income tax by $100 if you qualify and have enough tax liability. It’s a way to plan your giving around tax season while helping a good cause.

These credits are nonrefundable: they do not create a refund beyond your tax liability. They may increase a refund of tax you already paid through withholding or estimated payments. Unused eligible credit may be carried forward for up to five years under the applicable program’s rules. A donation is not a savings deposit, and the credit does not replace any required estimated-tax payments.

Check the official tables for the tax year you plan to claim. The limits apply across all gifts in each credit category, not separately to every organization. Splitting STO donations between ACSTO and Arizona Tuition Connection does not double your STO limit. The same gift cannot be counted for multiple Arizona credits.

A simple giving checklist

  1. Estimate your Arizona income-tax liability and choose your tax year.
  2. Check the credit limits for your filing status and any credits you have already used.
  3. Donate directly to the STO, eligible public school, certified QCO, or certified QFCO you want to support.
  4. Keep your receipts and complete the applicable Arizona credit forms. Review the contribution deadline and tax-year election rules; qualifying gifts made by April 15 may be eligible for the preceding tax year.

This is general information, not personalized tax advice. A tax professional can help you coordinate these credits with your tax liability, other credits, and any federal deduction rules.
