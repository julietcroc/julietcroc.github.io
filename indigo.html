How to Support Indigo with a Tax Credit

Eligible Arizona contributions may provide a dollar-for-dollar state income-tax credit, subject to applicable limits and your tax liability.

  1. Choose a School Tuition Organization and make your contribution.
  2. Recommend Indigo Randolph for scholarship consideration.
  3. If asked for his school, select Valley Christian Schools.

How the Arizona tax credit works

A credit reduces eligible Arizona income tax dollar for dollar. It is not a savings account or a replacement for required tax payments. These credits are nonrefundable; unused eligible credit may be carried forward under the program’s rules.

Check the limits for your tax year and filing status before donating. Original and Switcher STO credits have separate requirements; giving through several STOs does not increase your total limit. Keep your receipts and follow the organization’s tax-year and filing instructions.

You may also qualify for separate credits for eligible public-school gifts and donations to certified charitable or foster care organizations, subject to each category’s rules and your tax liability.

Meet Indigo

Faith, Friendships, and a Future at Valley

We chose Christian education for Indigo because we wanted him to build a foundation rooted in Christ, one he could rely on even when other areas of life felt uncertain. When he transitioned from his Christian daycare to Valley Christian, he was able to stay connected with many of his childhood friends.

Five years later, those friendships and that faith-centered community remain important parts of his life.

Indigo also loves athletics! This season, we've been blessed to find Legends Baseball, where he has grown tremendously as a player. He continues to be a power hitter and has made wonderful progress as a pitcher. Comfortable nearly anywhere on the field, he primarily plays pitcher and third base, while also serving as a backup shortstop.

He also loves playing soccer with his friends and has shown a natural aptitude for goalkeeper and defender.

Our hope is that Indigo will continue at Valley through junior high and high school, with the opportunity to represent his school in the athletic programs he already dreams of joining.

Your support helps us continue investing in the faith, friendships, and opportunities that are shaping who Indigo is becoming.
